Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Makra is a nutrition app for runners. This policy explains what the app collects, why it is used, where it is kept, who processes it, and the choices you have. In short: we use your information to run Makra for you, and nothing about you is ever sold, advertised against, or shared for marketing.

Who operates Makra

Makra is built and operated by Ryan Egerton, an independent developer based in the United States ("Makra", "we", or "us"). For privacy questions or requests, email hello@makraapp.com.

What you give us

To work out your daily targets, Makra needs to know some things about you: your date of birth, sex, height, weight, everyday activity level, your typical easy running pace, and the units you prefer. As you use the app you add more – the runs you log, the food you log, meals you plan for a future day, and meals you save as favourites.

We use this information to calculate your daily calorie and macronutrient targets, show your history, and back up and sync your plan across your devices. The nutrition calculations happen on your device, by rules, not by a service somewhere else.

Your account

Makra requires an account, created with Sign in with Apple. It is the only way to sign in, and it is what your data is attached to – without it, a new or replaced phone would mean starting over.

If you use Apple's Hide My Email, Makra only ever receives the relay address Apple generates. We never see your real email address, and the app does not email you.

Where your data is kept

Your profile, food logs, runs, planned meals, favourites and consent record are stored in Makra's database, hosted by Supabase in the United Kingdom. Encrypted copies of that database are taken nightly and deleted after 14 days.

This exists so your information survives a lost, broken or replaced phone – not so that anyone can look at it. Access is restricted to the account it belongs to.

Subscriptions and payment

Makra is a paid app. Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through your App Store account. Makra never sees your card details, billing address or payment information – Apple does not share them with us.

We use RevenueCat to keep track of whether a subscription is active, restore access on a new device, and understand subscription performance. RevenueCat receives your Makra account identifier and subscription and purchase status. It does not receive your profile, food logs, runs, planned meals, favourites, or searched location.

Food and restaurant lookups

When you scan a barcode, Makra sends that barcode to Open Food Facts and may check USDA FoodData Central when no match is found. The request does not include your Makra profile, logs or account identifier.

When you ask Eat Out to find nearby places, Makra uses your location for that search. Approximate coordinates are sent through Makra's service to OpenStreetMap, and restaurant or food search terms are sent to FatSecret. Makra does not attach your profile, logs or account identifier to those requests, and it does not save the searched location to your account.

Crash reports

When the app crashes or hits an error, an automatic report is sent to Sentry so it can be fixed. Sentry also receives limited session-health information, such as whether an app session ended in a crash, so we can measure reliability. Reports contain technical detail such as device model, iOS version, app version, and where in the code the failure happened. They are stripped of account and request information and never intentionally include your profile, food logs, runs, or location.

Service providers and protection

Makra uses Apple, Supabase, RevenueCat, Sentry, Open Food Facts, USDA FoodData Central, OpenStreetMap, and FatSecret for the specific purposes described above. These providers may process information in countries outside your own, including the United States and United Kingdom.

We require service providers acting on our behalf to use information only to provide their service to Makra and to protect it to the same or an equivalent standard as this policy and applicable privacy law. We do not allow them to use Makra health or fitness information for advertising, marketing, or data-broker activity.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data, to anyone, for any purpose.
  • We never share your health information for advertising or marketing.
  • There is no advertising in Makra, and no advertising identifiers.
  • There is no behavioural or advertising analytics – we do not record what you tap or which Makra features you use. RevenueCat provides subscription analytics, and Sentry provides limited crash and session-health diagnostics.
  • We do not build a profile of you beyond what you have entered yourself.

Why we process information

Where privacy law requires a legal basis, we process account, profile, nutrition, fitness, subscription, and lookup information because it is needed to provide the Makra service you request. We rely on consent where required for health-related information and device permissions, on our legitimate interest in keeping Makra secure and reliable for limited diagnostics, and on legal obligations where records must be kept.

How long information is kept

  • Your Makra account, profile, logs, runs, planned meals, favourites, and consent record are kept while your account remains open.
  • When account deletion completes, those records are removed from Makra's live database. Encrypted database backups expire within 14 days.
  • Makra does not save your Eat Out location or lookup history to your account.
  • Limited Sentry diagnostic reports are kept only as long as reasonably needed to investigate reliability and security problems, according to the retention configured for the service.
  • Apple and RevenueCat may retain transaction or subscription records under their own legal obligations and privacy terms. Deleting Makra does not erase purchase records Apple is required or permitted to keep.

Deleting your data

Two options live in Settings, and they do different things:

  • Sign out removes Makra data from the iPhone you are holding. Your account, cloud backup and Apple subscription are kept, so signing in again restores your data.
  • Delete account & all data erases your Makra data from the phone and the live Makra database, deletes the account itself, and disconnects Sign in with Apple. It cannot be undone. Encrypted database backups expire within 14 days, and transaction records held by Apple or RevenueCat are handled separately as described above.

You can export a spreadsheet (CSV) of your data first, from the same screen – profile, food log, planned meals, runs and favourites. Deleting the app from your phone removes the local copy but does not delete the account – use "Delete account & all data" for that.

Deleting a Makra account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Subscriptions are managed separately by Apple; cancel in your App Store account settings to stop future renewals and charges. Cancelling a subscription does not delete your Makra data.

Your choices and privacy rights

  • Access and portability: use Export data in Settings to save a CSV copy of your profile, food log, planned meals, runs, and favourites.
  • Correction: edit your profile and logged information in the app.
  • Location: allow or withdraw location access at any time in iOS Settings. Makra only requests it when you use Eat Out.
  • Deletion and withdrawal: use Delete account & all data to close your account and stop future account syncing, or email us if you cannot reach the app.
  • Other requests: depending on where you live, you may also ask us to restrict or object to processing. You may complain to your local privacy or data-protection authority.

Email hello@makraapp.com to exercise a privacy right. We may need to verify that the account is yours before acting on a request.

Device backups

If you have iCloud Backup or another device backup enabled, your device may include Makra's on-device data in your own encrypted backup. That backup is controlled by you and your device platform, not by Makra.

Children

Makra is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Not medical advice

Makra provides general nutrition and fitness guidance for educational purposes only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Consult a qualified healthcare professional before making changes to your diet or exercise routine.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected here and in the in-app Privacy & Data screen, and the date at the top will change.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or want your data deleted and can't reach the app? Email hello@makraapp.com.
